## 1.15.0 — 2026-10-08 (release R2026.16, owner review)
* **Sequencing depth for every sample.** `gut_sample_metadata_wide` gains `n_runs`, `seq_gbp` (bases summed over the sample's runs / 1e9), `seq_reads` and `seq_depth_source` (ena | ncbi_sra); `gut_runs` gains `base_count_source` and `run_study_accession`. ENA reports base_count 0 for runs whose files it does not mirror (dbGaP-protected deposits such as TEDDY PRJNA400115, very recent submissions); those now take NCBI SRA runinfo bases (`scripts/fill_run_bases.py`, cached in data/inputs/registry/run_bases_ncbi.parquet). Samples without a run in their own study take the same BioSample's runs filed under another study, then the curated infant runs table. `gut_studies.gbp_per_sample_median` added.
* **Registry: why a study is or is not in the catalog.** Every registry row and detail panel states the failing criteria of the catalog rule (host not human / not established, assay not a DNA shotgun metagenome with the library source and strategy, no gut samples, owner decision, pending).
* **Fields page:** the per-age-category cells of the `age_category` row now show the share of each category that rests on an exact age (they read 100 % by definition before).
* **Site:** navigation Home · Sample sheet · Project sheet · Contribute · Explore ▾ (Registry, Cohorts, Collections, Authors, Insights: Atlas) · About ▾ (About, For LLMs & API, Downloads & releases, Scope, Methods, Fields, Sources); minimalist home page (description, one search box that also matches conditions and interventions, example queries, links); Project-sheet filters — health condition × intervention × design × age × country × "cases & healthy controls" × "treated & control arms", all URL-addressable; Sample sheet shows depth and intervention arm.
* **For LLMs & API:** `llms.txt` (root), `llms/index.html` with a copy-paste prompt, and static endpoints `api/index.json`, `api/projects.json`, `api/by-intervention/<code>.json`, `api/by-condition/<code>.json`, `api/by-age/<category>.json`, `api/vocabularies.json`, `api/samples/<ACCESSION>.csv`.
* **Analytics:** `config/site.yaml analytics` (GoatCounter, GA4 or Plausible); off until an id is set.
