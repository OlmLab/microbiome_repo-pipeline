## 1.16.0 — 2026-10-08 (release R2026.17, owner review part 2)
* **Pending and unresolved registry studies classified.** 657 human studies that were `pending` or had assay `unknown` (plus gut-worded ones) went through the registry classifier (two Sonnet-class replicates, Opus-class adjudication on disagreement; 31 remain pending). Six enter the gut catalog (PRJEB6358 human samples only, PRJNA1441102, PRJNA1501268, PRJNA783956, PRJNA804967, PRJNA895415); PRJNA1501268 was recoded `other` → `shotgun_dna` from ENA run metrics (METAGENOMIC source, 151 bp PE NovaSeq X, ≈ 5.5 Gbp/run). Five catalog studies that had entered on pending defaults leave (16S amplicon, cancer cell-line, isolate genomes, or no gut evidence): PRJEB96943, PRJNA1273462, PRJNA1377467, PRJNA1457996, PRJNA755511.
* **Host / assay audit of the catalog** (`classification_stage = curator_audit`, `config/registry_overrides.yaml`, report docs/HOST_AUDIT_REPORT.md). The 37 catalog studies with animal words or "Genome sequencing and assembly" in their titles were checked against run and BioSample fields: 4 studies leave (PRJNA1082665 dogs/cats, PRJNA1337640 mice, PRJNA1240831 and PRJNA609594 in-vitro cultures) and the non-human, non-gut, culture and control samples of 26 others are removed through `config/sample_exclusions.csv` (2,104 samples; mice, pigs, dogs, cats, gorillas, chickens, horses, urine, skin, reagent controls). Three classifier-eligible studies were found to be isolate collections during the BioSample harvest and are held out the same way (PRJNA1255389, PRJNA1258574, PRJNA631464).
* **Infant-catalog samples get the gut_all routes.** BioSample attributes harvested for all 153,845 infant-catalog BioSamples (`gut_r1_infant_v1`; agreement with the infant curation: sex 100 %, country 99.6 %, age 99.2 %), abstract/description statements for the 389 infant studies (`gut_r4_infant_v1`), and gut_all study-level statements (R3b/R4b) now expand to infant samples for fields other than age and the infant-only fields. The infant curation still wins wherever it has a value. Coverage gradient documented on Methods → Limitations ("Known bias").
* **Docs:** DATA_DICTIONARY / README qualify the infant-era `triage_verdict`, `catalog_status` and `reason_code` columns (they describe the infant extension, not gut_all membership).
* **Site:** Contribute page reduced to three steps and one "Share metadata" button per project, opening a short issue form (`share-metadata.yml`: accession, what you are sharing, link or file, sample-ID column, CC-BY checkbox); registry table shows ✓ / — for catalog membership with the reason in the detail panel only.
