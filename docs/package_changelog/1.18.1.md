## 1.18.1 — 2026-10-09 (release R2026.21, deep annotation of the R2026.20 samples; owner audit of PRJEB120967)
* **Deep annotation of the 15,457 samples added in 1.18.0 that remain in the catalog** (13 sub-agent shards, same method as R2026.18: every BioSample attribute, two-replicate sample-name decoding, linked papers' body and supplementary tables joined by identifier, cohort-wide statements from the paper or archive description). Coverage on these samples, 1.18.0 → 1.18.1: subject 0 → 58 %, timepoint 0 → 36 %, exact age 12 → 27 %, sex 14 → 29 %, health condition 10 → 66 %, BMI 0 → 10 %, antibiotics 2 → 26 %, country 81 → 92 %, collection date 3 → 55 %. Agreement with published values where both exist: sex and country 100 %; the age disagreements were published errors (below).
* **85 new paper links for 60 projects** (66 own-data, 19 re-use; accession in full text, BioProject link or reasoning-model-verified title match) and model-written short titles / descriptions for 57 projects.
* **Never-downloaded BioSamples harvested** (owner audit: PRJEB120967 showed 0 samples). 3,299 DNA BioSamples of 37 catalog projects had no archive record in the registry tables; 3,243 were fetched, 54 private PRJEB80390 BioSamples enter from their public run records. PRJEB120967 (32), PRJEB120968 (66), PRJEB120969 (36) and PRJEB80390 (54) now have samples. A new release check (`scripts/check_unharvested.py`) fails when a catalog project has DNA BioSamples that are neither catalog samples, exclusions nor harvested.
* **Removed:** 458 control samples published in 1.18.0 (364 American Gut blanks in PRJEB11419, 88 KatharoSeq dilutions in PRJEB83236, 5 water blanks in PRJNA477357, 1 bioreactor sample in PRJNA1145114), plus, among the newly harvested records, bacterial isolate genomes (e.g. 664 in PRJEB63570), mouse / livestock / wastewater samples, mock communities, 392 amplicon-only oral samples of PRJEB77729 and 16 blood-culture isolates of PRJNA947616 (`config/sample_exclusions.csv`).
* **Audited corrections** (`config/deep_corrections.csv`, applied by `integrate_deep.py --corrections`): infant ages that the archive gives without a unit or in months had been read as years (PRJNA1148144 day of life; PRJNA1067625 months; PRJEB123118 month tokens; PRJNA1249435 '12 months' normalised to 30 d); 10 mis-normalised '<n> months' age pairs fixed in `registry_pairs_normalised`.
* Known gaps: PDF-only supplements were mostly not parsed; several papers are not open access (abstract only); run-unit samples of split BioSamples still have no Sandpiper profile.
