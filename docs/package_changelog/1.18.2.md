## 1.18.2 — 2026-10-09 (release R2026.22, owner audit of empty projects and of split deposits)
* **Catalog projects without samples removed (25).** A catalog project must now have at least one catalog sample (`build_gut_scope`, logged as `empty_projects_dropped` in the scope summary). Removed: 20 umbrella children whose single BioSample is catalogued under the parent BioProject (MetaHIT subject projects under PRJNA33049; PRJEB80526 → PRJEB101484; PRJNA871195 → PRJNA869587; PRJNA888226 → PRJNA880658); PRJEB39960 (all 87 BioSamples are mouse recipients of human faecal microbiota; registry host set to `no` by a curator-audit override); PRJNA1401443, SRP005975 and SRP006081 (no harvestable runs in the registry run table: Salmonella isolate genomes with mislabelled metagenomes, an SRA-only culturomics record, gnotobiotic-mouse culture collections). All stay in the registry.
* **Study groups.** BioProjects that are deposits of one study (per-subject deposits, split or top-up deposits, identical records, a shared own-data paper) are linked by `gut_studies.study_group_id` / `study_group_basis` / `study_group_members` / `study_group_n_samples` (`config/study_groups.csv`; 55 groups proposed from identical records, title templates, near-identical titles of one submitter and shared papers, each partition confirmed by two independent reasoning-model replicates with a verified quote; 51 groups / 123 projects remain after the empty-project rule). Project pages show the group with links; the Project sheet marks grouped projects and filters a group with `?group=<id>`. Projects stay accession-keyed (no merge).
