## 1.19.0 — 2026-10-10 (release R2026.23, subject ids, longitudinal design and relatedness)
* **Catalog-wide subject_id pass.** Every project with subject_id coverage < 95 % (1,671 projects) was re-read: archive attributes, sample-name decoding, author repositories / supplementary tables where reachable, and the papers' design statements. 1,423 projects were processed (7 of 48 shards, ≈ 250 projects, were stopped by the model provider's content filter and were not retried). subject_id now covers 437,669 samples (72.9 %; was 322,008, 53.6 %): 36,371 samples gained a recovered per-person id and 81,031 a **generated** id.
* **Generated subject ids (new rule, docs/DEEP_ANNOTATION_BRIEF.md › SUBJECT RULE).** When the paper or record states one sample per person (or a single-subject case study), the id is minted by rule: `subject_id = sample_key` (`determined_by gut_subject_unique_v1`) or the project accession for a single subject (`gut_subject_single_v1`, `gut_subject_single_case_v1`); `parse_note` starts with `generated:` and carries the basis. Generated ids pass the subject_id route gate although their evidence is R3/R4 text; they are excluded from source counts and from longitudinal statistics, and are marked "generated" on sample pages.
* **Merged subject ids split.** The relatedness pass flagged published subject ids that merge people (mother–infant dyad or couple ids, FMT procedure / donor numbers, placeholders such as `stool`, `unique`, diagnoses used as ids). 3,575 samples were re-identified (recode, `determined_by gut_subject_fix_v1`) and 1,741 lost an id that names no person (retire); 1,830 further proposals that the agents marked for review are not applied (`data/inputs/gut/design/logs/subject_id_fixes_shard_*.csv`).
* **Longitudinal design.** New wide columns `subject_n_samples`, `days_since_first_sample` (age, else full collection date, else parsed timepoint label), `time_axis`, `person_key`. New gut_studies columns `n_subjects`, `n_subjects_repeated`, `n_samples_repeated`, `samples_per_subject_median/max`, `followup_days_median/max`, `interval_days_median`, `time_axis`, `longitudinal_status` (longitudinal 1,143 · cross_sectional 1,153 · repeated_unresolved 189 · unknown 373) and the stated design from a study-design pass over 2,385 projects (`longitudinal_design`, `sampling_schedule`, `planned_offsets_days`, `time_origin`, `followup_days_stated`; 7 of 40 shards were stopped by the content filter).
* **Relatedness and shared settings.** New pack fields `family_id`, `family_role` (config/vocab/family_roles.yaml), `shared_setting_type` (config/vocab/shared_settings.yaml), `shared_setting_id`; new tables `gut_relatedness.parquet` (5,858 groups: 5,230 families, 628 shared settings) and `gut_relations.parquet` (1,015 stated pairwise relations: FMT donor_of, twins, partners, roommates, mother_of …). gut_studies gains `n_families`, `n_related_people`, `n_mother_child_families`, `n_twin_sets`, `n_shared_settings`, `family_motifs`, `shared_setting_types`, `relatedness_designs`, `relatedness_summary`; 281 projects carry a relatedness design (mother–infant 79, FMT donor → recipient 77, households 58, twins 25 …).
* **Site.** Project pages get a "Sampling design" section (longitudinal badges and statistics, schedule, per-person swimlane timeline, family motif glyphs, settings and a families table); the Project sheet has a Sampling filter (longitudinal, mother–infant, parent–child, twins, siblings, households, communities, institutions, FMT); minimal **sample pages** (`samples/sample.html?id=<sample>`, one static page reading 100 lookup shards) show every value with its route, evidence and the person's other samples; About › Where values come from now counts **facts** (one per sample × concept; location fields and date grouped, identifiers excluded) and documents subjects, generated ids, the longitudinal derivation and the relatedness fields.
