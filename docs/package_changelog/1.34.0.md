# Package 1.34.0 (R2026.41)

Health-condition labels now say what the samples are, per-sample case and control labels were read from archive fields for 115
projects, per-sample values were added from supplementary tables and from metadata files in repositories the papers name, and
the registry was refreshed. `RELEASE_NOTES_R2026.41.md` has the per-field counts.

## Health-condition labels that say what the samples are

- 11,049 profiled stool samples carried a health-condition code whose stored detail was a group code, an abbreviation or the
  value of another field, so that a reader could not tell which condition, or which kind of control, the samples are. The 146
  (project, label) pairs with at least five analysable persons were read against the project's own texts by two independent
  Opus 5 readers, and by a third where the two differed (22 pairs). Every decision quotes the passage it rests on.
- **121 pairs (9,364 samples) get an explicit detail**; for 1,028 of these samples the code changes as well, because the agreeing
  readings name another code (for example hospital controls that had been coded as healthy controls). 4 pairs (596 samples) lose
  a health condition that the label never stated and the project gives no other basis for. 21 pairs are unchanged: the readers
  could not decide (9), the label is not a condition but the code has another basis (8), a trial-cohort code is kept (3), three
  readings differ (1).
- A further reader audited 31 of the agreed pairs: 20 of 20 random pairs right; of 11 pairs whose notes mentioned mixed people, 9
  right, 1 with a better detail (used) and 1 wrong (a withdrawal of a trial-cohort code; not applied, and such codes are no
  longer withdrawn).

## Case and control labels read from archive fields (label hunt)

- 259 projects whose paper or archive record compares people with a condition against controls, while no catalogued field
  separated the groups, were searched by an Opus 5 reader for an archive attribute, a run field or a pattern in the sample names
  whose meaning the project's own text states. A second reader checked every proposal: where the meaning is stated, whether the
  group sizes agree with the text, whether the groups are groups of people by health condition, and re-derived the label of every
  sample.
- **115 projects are written (20,946 samples)**: 4,225 samples gain or change a health-condition code and the others gain an
  explicit detail. 36 of the projects have cases and healthy controls, 6 cases and other controls, 68 cases only and 5 healthy
  controls only. For 12 projects no text spells the labels out and the mapping is fixed by the stated group sizes (exact and
  unique match); these carry a lower confidence.
- Not written: 133 projects without a basis, 4 rejected by the second reader, 6 whose labels are right but whose proposed
  wording was not, and one deposit of in-vitro material.
- These values are checked corrections (`determined_by` = `dq_adjudication_v2:r41`); the decision tables are in the pipeline under
  `docs/audits/r41/`.

## Registry refresh

- Deposits made public since 25 October 2026: 43 new candidate projects, 30 admitted after two readings.
- New enumeration slice for metagenomic libraries of human-associated communities filed under an unusual library strategy
  (POOLCLONE, CLONE, CLONEEND, FINISHING, Synthetic-Long-Read, WCS): 64 new candidates, 18 read as in scope, 6 admitted — those
  whose median run has at least 0.5 Gbp. The other 12 have run sizes typical of amplicon data and wait for a read check.

## Per-sample values from supplementary tables and repository files

- **Supplementary tables of newly linked papers.** 1,974 projects with a paper package on disk had never been tested for a
  table that joins their samples; 424 join. After column classification, value mapping, the cross-check against archive values
  and a whole-column check of every health, age and life-stage column (4 health columns dropped, 2 kept in part), 43,625 values
  for 118 projects are added (route R2). Most repeat what the catalog already had; cells that were empty before: time point
  6,488, sex 828, country 626, BMI 368, age 209, health condition 87.
- **Metadata files in repositories named in data-availability statements.** 2,414 projects have such a statement; 826 links to
  GitHub (503 repositories listed), Zenodo (216 records) and figshare (50 items) were followed and 1,021 metadata-like tables
  taken from 226 repositories. 127 projects have a table column that matches at least half of their samples. After the same
  checks (7 health columns dropped and 13 kept in part, 1 age column dropped; a spot check of 45 values found all right),
  32,598 values for 66 projects are added. Cells that were empty before: sex 5,647, BMI 4,832, time point 2,588, health
  condition 683, subject 468, age 209.
- Not used: sex values that are neither female nor male; all values of one project and the health conditions of three others,
  where the table disagrees with the catalog's existing health conditions for more than one sample in ten; 15 figshare items
  whose files could not be downloaded.

## Site

- Atlas: three territories without a UNDP Human Development Index take a value from another published source, each recorded
  with its source in `country_hdi.csv` — Taiwan 0.934 (2023; national statistics office, UNDP method), Puerto Rico 0.845 (2015;
  published calculation with the UNDP method), French Guiana 0.799 (2023; Global Data Lab subnational HDI). The Turks and Caicos
  Islands and the British Indian Ocean Territory stay without a value. The Atlas observation cards were not recomputed.
