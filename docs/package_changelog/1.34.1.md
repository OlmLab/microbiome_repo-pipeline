# Package 1.34.1 (R2026.42)

A site release. No sample, project or value was added, changed or retired: `catalog_sample_metadata_wide` is identical to
that of 1.34.0 row for row, and `RELEASE_NOTES_R2026.42.md` shows no change in any field. Three pages are new on the site, the
section that was called "Atlas" is now "Insights", and the menu was reordered.

What does differ from 1.34.0 inside the tables is bookkeeping, found by comparing every table row for row: the profile
tables are recomputed in every release and differ in the last decimal places (by at most 4e-8 in any value); the rows of
`catalog_rejected_values` and `catalog_sample_determinations_conflicts` carry this release in `release_added`; 320
health-condition rows carry this release's name in their `parse_note`; one project note in `study_metadata_wide` grew by a
repeated audit line. The last three are defects of the build, not changes to the data, and are listed for the next release.

## Site

- **Taxon explorer** (`atlas/taxa/index.html`): one page for every genus and species that the community profiles detect in
  QC-clean samples — 8,392 genera and 24,324 species from 379,776 samples — as a GTDB R232 taxonomy list sorted by how widely
  each name is carried, and as the GTDB reference tree with its branch lengths. A taxon opens over the full width with its
  prevalence and abundance by body site, age group, country and lifestyle, a map, the projects that carry it and, for 428
  taxa, a literature summary written by an AI model and labelled so. A click on a bar lists the samples of that group in which
  the taxon was detected; the separate link to the sample table shows every sample of the group.
- **VANISH / BloSSUM** (`atlas/vanish.html`): every tested species, genus and family placed by how much more common it is in
  non-industrialized or in industrialized people, under a model rule (cohort as a random effect, sequencing depth as a
  covariate, p-values from cohort-label permutations, leave-one-cohort-out) and under a simple rule on country HDI bands;
  adults and infants separately. A third class, "StIL candidate", is the prevalence half of the published definition only.
  Infants under the model rule use the broad definition of non-industrialized and rest on few cohorts; the page says so. The
  method text and every per-taxon table can be downloaded from the page.
  In answer to a published critique of how such lists are made and read (Ibata et al., bioRxiv 2026.11.17.767315), the page
  states six points on how to read the lists — among them that the names describe a difference between groups and not a
  disappearance, and that the simple rule adjusts for neither sequencing depth nor study — and has a collapsed section
  "Robustness" for exploratory checks of the classes; the longer checks that have not been done are listed there. No class
  and no threshold was changed.
- **Replication of published findings** (`atlas/replication.html`): the results of the pre-registered replication test (plan
  frozen before the run, hash `d5f56cba23415d8dcaa8ff8702319035e71d3810c3f45f2dba04d86015c6a966`; run on package 1.34.0). Of
  6,933 published claims that a gut taxon is more or less abundant in people with a condition than in healthy controls, 1,918
  could be tested on this catalog and 515 of them replicated (26.9 %). The page shows every condition, every claim and every
  project estimate behind a claim, with the plan, the report and the tables as downloads. The test was not repeated for this
  release: its results belong to the package that was under test.
- The three pages were computed on package 1.34.0 (R2026.41) and say so; 1.34.1 holds the same data.
- **Menu**: Home · Projects · Samples · Contribute · Insights · About. Insights holds the taxon explorer, VANISH / BloSSUM,
  the replication page, the taxon map and the PCA of community profiles. Registry, Cohorts, Collections and Authors moved
  into the About menu under "Browse"; the Explore menu is gone.
- The name "Atlas" is no longer used on the site: menu, page titles, breadcrumbs and sub-navigation say "Insights" or name the
  page. Page addresses did not change (the pages stay under `atlas/`), so existing links work. Entries of earlier releases in
  this changelog keep the old name.
- "Profile measures" (`atlas/measures.html`) and "Figures" (`atlas/figures.html`) are no longer in the menu; both pages stay
  at their addresses.
- Literature references in the taxon explorer link to `doi.org` (added to the hosts a script may link to; nothing is loaded
  from it).
