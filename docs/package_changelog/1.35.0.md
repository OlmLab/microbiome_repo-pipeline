# Package 1.35.0 (R2026.44)

A data release. No project or sample was added. Condition labels were revised and given disease names with ontology
identifiers, and values that a comparison with another resource and a blind audit had questioned were corrected where the
cited source, read again, gave a different value. Every corrected value keeps its source, locator and quote in
`catalog_sample_determinations.parquet` (`determined_by = dq_adjudication_v2:r44`); the value it replaces is kept as a retired
row.

## New columns

`catalog_sample_metadata_wide.parquet` has three columns after `health_condition_detail`:

| column | content |
|---|---|
| `health_condition_name` | name of the disease or condition that the sample's label states |
| `health_condition_efo_id` | identifier of that term in the Experimental Factor Ontology (EFO 3.96.0) |
| `health_condition_mondo_id` | MONDO identifier of the same condition where EFO links one (MONDO 2026-11-03) |

The three are derived from the pair (`health_condition`, `health_condition_detail`) through `config`-level table
`condition_terms.csv` (6,450 rows, 784 conditions); they are not separate determinations, and their evidence is that of the
label. They are empty for healthy controls, for labels that describe a cohort, an exposure group or a trial arm rather than a
condition, and for labels whose text names no condition. EFO takes many disease terms from MONDO, HP and Orphanet and keeps
their identifiers, so most values of `health_condition_efo_id` start with `MONDO:`.

## Condition labels

- **Unclear and unsettled strings settled**: 684 (project, code, detail) pairs were read again against the project record
  and the paper; the detail was rewritten to what the source says and, for 163 pairs, the code changed with it.
- **Free text in `health_condition`**: the 17 strings that stood in the coded column (3,603 samples) now carry a code of
  the vocabulary; the free text is kept at the start of `health_condition_detail`.
- Together these are 701 pairs, 15,753 samples in 199 projects (`docs/audits/r44/condition_pair_changes_r44.csv` in the
  pipeline repository). One pair was withdrawn: PRJEB63360 (230 samples), where the only source is the description of an
  occupational exposure cohort that names no infection.
- **Case / control fixes** after the comparison of case and control labels with a published curated collection (Degregori
  et al., iMeta 2026): 160 samples in 4 projects (`condition_sample_changes_r44.csv`).
- **Left out and listed** (`conditions_left_out_r44.csv`): 52 flagged pairs (833 samples) that the project record did not
  settle, and one pair of PRJNA480674 (6 samples) whose proposed label needs a vocabulary code that does not exist.

## Values corrected after the comparison with Metalog and the blind audit

Metalog (EMBL) was used for validation only: where its value and the catalog's differed in a consistent way, the cited source
of the catalog value was read again for every sample of the pattern, and the catalog changed only where that source gave
another value. No Metalog value was copied. The audit rows are single sampled values; each was checked alone and no fix was
extended to other samples.

| field | samples changed | note |
|---|---:|---|
| `body_site_code` | 2,379 | PRJEB45799 saliva (1,929, was gut); PRJNA1085219, PRJNA475246, PRJNA949528 stool; PRJNA352475 oral, skin and vaginal sites; PRJNA384716 milk; PRJEB28097 duodenal mucosa |
| `subject_id` | 1,789 | PRJEB32631: 1,415 samples of 507 people (was one person per sample); PRJNA510445, PRJNA63661, PRJNA289586, PRJNA384716, PRJNA947377 |
| `age_at_collection_days` | 225 | PRJNA1112983, PRJEB74322 |
| `country` | 222 | PRJEB42363 Malawi (177), PRJEB21933 United Kingdom (40), PRJNA63661 (5) |
| `health_condition` | 220 | PRJNA385949, PRJNA1112983, PRJNA277905, PRJEB25422, PRJEB44237 and 10 audit rows |
| `sex` | 176 | PRJNA1112983, PRJEB25422, PRJEB74322 |
| `sample_type` | 127 | blanks of PRJNA266117 and PRJEB53403 marked as negative controls (they lose their person fields) |
| other fields | 54 | delivery mode 12, collection date 11, intervention detail 8, preterm status 7, condition detail 5, NEC status 2, nine fields with one value each |

5,192 values of 4,972 samples in 54 projects (`confirmed_fixes_r44.csv`): 5,145 from 36 Metalog patterns and 47 from the
audit. `body_site_code` and `sample_type` are not determinations; their corrections are in `config/body_site_corrections.csv`
and `config/sample_types.csv` of the pipeline repository, each row with its source and quote, and `body_site_basis` says
whether the code comes from the sample's own record or from the project's statement.

**Not applied and listed** (`fixes_not_applied_r44.csv`, 432 rows): 316 rows that carry a check flag (16 Metalog patterns,
300 audit rows); 107 audit rows where the cited source alone does not support the value (no value was removed in this
release); 7 Metalog patterns that the source did not confirm (five of them are a years-to-days conversion that differs by a
day or two, not a wrong age) and 2 rows whose source could not be opened.

## Site

- **Condition explorer** (`atlas/conditions.html`, Insights): every condition with its EFO / MONDO identifier, the projects
  and samples that carry it, body sites, countries, age bands, and the published associations tested on the replication page.
- **Samples**: filter by disease name (`?condition=`); `api/by-condition/` has one file per condition identifier.
- **Replication of published findings**: an exploratory section was added below the registered result, which is unchanged.
  The page states that condition labels were revised after the registered run.
- **External resources**: Metalog added.
- **Fields**: the text on confidence follows the blind audit.
- VANISH / BloSSUM and the replication results keep the results of package 1.34.0 and say so.
