**One catalog, two tiers, all ages.** The **catalog** tables (`catalog_*`) are the curated tier: human-associated microbial communities sequenced by shotgun DNA or RNA sequencing, from every body site and at every age — **{n_gut_studies:,} projects, {n_gut_samples:,} samples, {n_gut_determinations:,} sample × field values** — with body site, age at collection, sex, BMI, country, health condition (controlled vocabulary), antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint recovered from the archive record, from the supplementary tables of linked papers, from the paper text and from abstracts; every value carries a verbatim evidence quote (≤ 12 words), the source it came from and a confidence. Each run is labelled DNA or RNA (`catalog_runs.nucleic_acid`), each sample says which data sets it has (`has_dna`, `has_rna`), and each project carries the nucleic acid of its data sets (`catalog_studies.nucleic_acid`). Coverage over all catalog samples: age {cov_age:.0%}, sex {cov_sex:.0%}, country {cov_country:.0%}, health condition {cov_hc:.0%}, antibiotic exposure {cov_abx:.0%}. The **registry** tables (`registry_*`) list every screened public study in ENA/SRA/DDBJ with a human-associated metagenome signal — {n_registry:,} studies — with archive-only classification (host, UBERON-anchored body sites, life stages, assay, access) and the harvested BioSample tier ({n_registry_biosamples:,} BioSamples with normalised site / age / sex / country).

**Start here.** `catalog_studies.parquet` (one row per project: body sites, DNA / RNA, papers, methods and setting read from the paper, per-field coverage, age-category and health-condition mix, curated depth) → `catalog_sample_metadata_wide.parquet` (one row per sample: every field with `__route` / `__confidence`, `body_site_code`, `age_category` + basis, `has_dna` / `has_rna`) → `catalog_sample_determinations.parquet` (the evidence behind every value) and `catalog_runs.parquet` (every run). `registry_studies.parquet` is the registry; `registry_runs_v{package_version}.parquet` (every screened run) ships as a GitHub Release asset.

**Birth and feeding fields.** The birth-cohort projects carry a deeper curation (delivery mode, feeding mode, preterm status, gestational age, birth weight, maternal antibiotics, probiotics, HMO supplementation, NEC). Those rows are in the `catalog_*` tables (`curated_source = infant_catalog`; `infant_scope = true` selects them: {n_infant_scope:,} samples), and the deeper tables of that curation (`sample_metadata_wide`, `sample_determinations`, `study_metadata_wide`, `cohorts`, `universe_studies_all`, `sandpiper_*`, …) are in the package as well.
