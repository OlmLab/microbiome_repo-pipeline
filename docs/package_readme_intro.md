**One catalog, two tiers, all ages.** The **catalog** tables (`catalog_*`) are the curated tier: human-associated microbial communities sequenced by shotgun DNA or RNA sequencing, from every body site and at every age — **{n_gut_studies:,} projects, {n_gut_samples:,} samples, {n_gut_determinations:,} sample × field values** — with body site, age at collection, sex, BMI, country, health condition (controlled vocabulary), antibiotic exposure, subject and timepoint recovered from archive attributes (R1), supplementary tables (R2), paper full text (R3) and abstracts (R4); every value carries a verbatim evidence quote (≤ 12 words), a labelled source, a route and a confidence. Each run is labelled DNA or RNA (`catalog_runs.nucleic_acid`), each sample says which data sets it has (`has_dna`, `has_rna`), and each project carries the nucleic acid of its data sets (`catalog_studies.nucleic_acid`). Coverage over all catalog samples: age {cov_age:.0%}, sex {cov_sex:.0%}, country {cov_country:.0%}, health condition {cov_hc:.0%}, antibiotic exposure {cov_abx:.0%}. The **registry** tables (`registry_*`) list every screened public study in ENA/SRA/DDBJ with a human-associated metagenome signal — {n_registry:,} studies — with archive-only classification (host, UBERON-anchored body sites, life stages, assay, access) and the harvested BioSample tier ({n_registry_biosamples:,} BioSamples with normalised site / age / sex / country).

**Start here.** `catalog_studies.parquet` (one row per project: body sites, DNA / RNA, papers, methods and setting read from the paper, per-field coverage, age-category and health-condition mix, curated depth) → `catalog_sample_metadata_wide.parquet` (one row per sample: every field with `__route` / `__confidence`, `body_site_code`, `age_category` + basis, `has_dna` / `has_rna`) → `catalog_sample_determinations.parquet` (the evidence behind every value) and `catalog_runs.parquet` (every run). `registry_studies.parquet` is the registry; `registry_runs_v{package_version}.parquet` (every screened run) ships as a GitHub Release asset.

**Infant extension.** The {n_infant_studies:,} infant gut projects carry a deeper infant-field curation (delivery mode, feeding mode, preterm status, gestational age, birth weight, maternal antibiotics, probiotics, HMO supplementation, NEC). Those rows are present in the `catalog_*` tables (`curated_source = infant_catalog`; `infant_scope = true` selects them: {n_infant_scope:,} samples) and the deep tables (`sample_metadata_wide`, `sample_determinations`, `study_metadata_wide`, `cohorts`, `universe_studies_all`, `sandpiper_*`, …) are in the package as well.
