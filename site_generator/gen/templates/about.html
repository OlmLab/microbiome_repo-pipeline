About this page

Two tiers. The registry lists every shotgun-metagenome study screened in ENA, NCBI SRA and DDBJ, classified for human host, body site, life stage, assay and access ({{ rs.n_human|fmt }} human studies of {{ rs.n_studies|fmt }}). The catalog is the curated part: human-associated shotgun DNA and RNA projects from every body site and every age ({{ stats.n_studies|fmt }} projects, {{ stats.n_samples|fmt }} samples). Every catalog value carries the quote it was read from, with its source and a confidence.

Scope
