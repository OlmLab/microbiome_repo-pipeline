    About this view — read before interpreting a map
    • Study is the master confounder. Most studies sample one country and one life stage with one protocol; a country's colour is the mixture of its few studies (click a country to see them). Compare countries only when the study list looks comparable, and prefer the per-study dossiers.
    • Repeated measures. Longitudinal cohorts contribute many samples per subject; n samples overstates the number of people.
    • Depth. Detection at ≥ 1e-4 depends on sequencing depth; shallow studies under-detect rare taxa (see the depth card on the observations page).
    • Which runs are profiled. Sandpiper profiles Illumina runs ≥ 100 Mbp public before its snapshot; runs failing its QC (non-metagenome, synthetic, RNA, predicted non-gut, root coverage < 2) and samples whose body site is not primary gut were excluded ({{ meta.sample_filter }}).
    • Taxonomy. {{ meta.taxonomy }}: names follow GTDB, so Prevotella copri is Prevotella, Bacteroides vulgatus is Phocaeicola vulgatus, and suffixed genera (Blautia_A) are GTDB placeholders.
    • Hong Kong and Singapore are drawn as dots (the 110 m base map merges them into larger polygons). Country is missing for {{ ((meta.n_samples - meta.n_samples_with_country) / meta.n_samples * 100) | round(1) }} % of samples.
    • Payload. data/atlas/taxa.json (index), countries.json, studies.json, matrix_<rank>.bin — format documented in taxa.json → meta.format; HDI values in country_hdi.csv ({{ meta.hdi_source }}; {{ meta.hdi_n_countries_with_value }} of {{ meta.n_countries }} atlas countries have a value).
