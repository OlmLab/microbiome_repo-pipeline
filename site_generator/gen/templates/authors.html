About this page

Each hit lists the catalog studies the author is linked to through a paper (Europe PMC author lists of the linked papers; for the infant-extension studies also the curated links). Each study appears as a preview card: accession, title, samples, country / top condition, year.

Caveats: names are string-matched from Europe PMC author strings — the same person can appear under several initial variants (e.g. "Aagaard K" and "Aagaard KM") and no disambiguation was attempted; studies without a linked paper have no authors; consortium names are kept as group authors. A paper linked with relation unsure may re-analyse rather than produce the data.
