Cohorts ({{ cohorts|length|fmt }})

A cohort groups catalog studies that belong together: BioProjects linked to the same publication (registry paper links: a paper that describes the data of 2–4 catalog studies, or a paper citing 2–4 studies that were all submitted by the same centre) or listed in the same curated cohort record (the infant-extension cohort resolution, which also supplies the cohort name).

About this page

{{ stats.n_studies_in_cohorts|fmt }} of the {{ stats.n_studies|fmt }} catalog studies belong to one of these {{ cohorts|length|fmt }} cohorts; the rest are single-deposit studies. Each cohort page lists its studies with sample counts, the shared papers and a merged sample download. The grouping is deterministic and is recomputed at every release from registry_study_papers, study_paper_links and cohorts.csv; the list is in catalog_cohorts.csv.
