What these numbers count

Counts are of samples whose health-condition label names the condition as the sampled person's main condition; each case sample counts under one condition only. Labels come from the projects' archive records, supplementary tables, paper text and abstracts; the split per source is on Fields & vocabularies. They were mapped to EFO {{ M.efo_version }} and MONDO ({{ M.mondo_release }}) by two or three independent readings. Some labels name a symptom or a finding rather than a disease ({{ n(M.n_phenotype) }} rows); these carry an HP or an Orphanet identifier.

Healthy controls are the samples labelled healthy in the projects that also hold cases of the condition; they are not matched controls. Cases counts every state of the condition together; the tooltip names the states that are recorded — current, treated or in remission, at risk or before onset, history of the condition — and the rest are not stated. Profiled counts the case samples with a community profile. Projects and Cases count the condition's own term; each condition's section also gives the totals including its narrower terms.

The three tables are rebuilt on each release from that release's labels and its condition terms; the ontology relations (broader and narrower terms) and every replication column are carried unchanged from the registered replication run, so they do not move with the catalog. The replication numbers are the registered result of the test run on {{ M.run_date }} (strict mapping, primary analysis): over the {{ n(M.n_tested) }} conditions tested in their own right, {{ n(M.claims_replicated) }} of {{ n(M.claims_testable) }} testable claims replicated. A row marked “tested as” a named broader condition shows the result for that broader condition, not for the row's own condition; {{ n(M.n_broader) }} rows are marked that way. The {{ n(M.n_associations) }} replicated associations of {{ M.n_assoc_conditions }} conditions are listed in each condition's section, and in full on Replication of published findings.

Limits

A condition with no row is not necessarily absent from the catalog. Counted on this release's labels, {{ n(M.labels_without_term) }} label strings — distinct pairs of a health-condition code and its detail text on current samples — name a condition for which no ontology term was found, and {{ n(M.labels_unsettled) }} are unclear or unsettled. Those samples are in the shipped tables under their own label, and on the pages of their projects.

The catalog is a convenience sample of public data, not a survey: the number of projects and samples for a condition says how much public data exists, not how common the condition is. Countries, age groups and body sites are curated from the projects' own records and are missing for some samples, which is why every row carries a “not stated” band. Healthy controls come from the same projects as the cases, so they are not matched on age, sex or country.
