External resources

Other teams have curated human microbiome metadata before us. Where their annotations join our samples through archive accessions, we include them with the source named on every value.

How these values are used

Values from these resources are evidence rows counted with the supplementary tables (evidence_source = external.<resource>); the locator points to the record at the source and the quote is the source's raw value. A sample's own archive record and the project's own supplementary tables take precedence. Please cite the resource's reference when you use values that carry its evidence source. Maintainers who prefer different terms can open an issue.
