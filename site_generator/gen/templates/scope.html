Scope: from the registry to the catalog

The registry is every study we screened in the public archives ({{ stats.n_registry|fmt }}). The catalog is the part of it we curated: {{ stats.n_studies|fmt }} projects with per-sample metadata. The catalog holds human-associated microbial communities sequenced by shotgun DNA or RNA sequencing, from every body site and at every age, plus sequenced mock and standard communities used as benchmarks. DNA and RNA data sets are kept apart: each run is labelled DNA or RNA, and each project carries the nucleic acid of its data sets.

A project is admitted when a classification of its archive record, sample attributes, read check and linked paper finds human-associated communities sequenced by shotgun. Out of scope are non-human hosts, environmental samples, amplicon sequencing of any gene (even when filed as WGS or RNA-Seq), human genome or human tissue transcriptome sequencing, single-cell libraries, cultured isolates and virus genome surveillance. Within an admitted project, amplicon and single-cell data sets are left out run by run, and a sample without any in-scope data set is left out.

About this page

Every sample of a catalog project is kept with its own body-site code (body_site_code; body_site_class = primary when a human site is stated, unknown when none is). A sample without a site attribute in a project that sampled one body site takes that site, with the basis recorded (body_site_basis = study_single_site).
