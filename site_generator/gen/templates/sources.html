External curated resources incorporated

Each resource, what it offers, how we join it and the projects that use it: External resources.

Other groups have curated human microbiome metadata before us. Where their per-sample or per-study annotations join our samples through archive accessions, we incorporate them as route R2 evidence rows — evidence_source = external.<resource>, the locator a link to the record at the source, the quote the source's raw value — after the study's own supplementary tables in precedence (a sample's own archive attribute always wins). We are grateful to these projects; please cite them when you use values that carry their evidence source.

Acknowledgement. We gratefully acknowledge the teams behind {% for r in src.related_efforts if r.ingestion == 'used' %}{{ r.name }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}: their curation work is incorporated here as route-R2 evidence, every incorporated value links back to its source record (evidence_source = external.<resource>), and users of those values should cite the reference listed for the resource alongside this catalog. Resources that publish no licence are redistributed with attribution only; if you maintain one of them and prefer different terms, open an issue and we will comply.
