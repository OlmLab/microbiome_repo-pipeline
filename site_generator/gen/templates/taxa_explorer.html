    How this is computed

    Community profiles come from Sandpiper (SingleM, GTDB {{ M.gtdb }}). A sample counts here when it is a specimen (not a control), has a profile, and carries none of these QC flags: low depth, non-metagenome organism label, synthetic, RNA library, ecological prediction, no genus assigned, low complexity. That leaves {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_samples) }} samples in {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_projects) }} projects; the other Insights pages use different subsets of the catalog, so their counts differ. Computed in release {{ site.release_id }} from data package {{ site.version }}.

    Prevalence is the share of samples in a group with a relative abundance above zero. Mean relative abundance averages over every sample in the group, counting undetected samples as zero. The body site chart is over all QC-clean samples; age group, country and lifestyle are computed within the samples of the taxon's leading body site, which is named above each of those charts. A group is drawn only with at least {{ M.min_cell_samples }} samples in at least {{ M.min_cell_projects }} projects; smaller groups are listed as “too few samples”. {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_no_leading_site) }} of the {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_taxa) }} taxa have no body site that passes the threshold and so have no age, country or lifestyle chart. This page shows no result by health condition.

    The tree is the GTDB {{ M.gtdb }} bacterial and archaeal reference tree pruned to these taxa, {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_nodes) }} nodes and {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_tips) }} tips, with every branch length preserved. Bacteria and Archaea are drawn as two trees, each with its own scale bar: the reference trees have no common root, so a distance read across the break would be meaningless. A genus with species in the catalog sits on the common ancestor of those species; {{ "{:,}".format(M.n_genus_only) }} genera with no species row sit on the tip of one representative genome.

    Limits

    The catalog is a convenience sample of public data, not a survey. Body site, age, country and lifestyle are curated from the projects’ own records and are missing for many samples. Relative abundances depend on the reference database, so a taxon with few reference genomes reads as less abundant than it is. Lifestyle groups are small and come from a handful of projects, and countries differ in which projects were run there, so a difference between groups is not by itself a difference between populations. {{ M.n_literature }} taxa carry a short summary written from the published literature by an AI model; those statements are published findings about the taxon, not results computed here.
